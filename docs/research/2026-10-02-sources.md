## openSUSE facts each item depends on

- **Packman:** the wiki recommends the Essentials repository only.
  `SDB:Installing_codecs_from_Packman_repositories`, snapshot 2026-05-11.
  `opi codecs` (opi 5.16.0) adds the full repository at priority 70;
  `opi/plugins/packman.py`. Slowroll has its own Packman tree.
- **Firefox H.264:** OpenH264 covers calls, not playback.
  `SDB:Firefox_MP4/H.264_Video_Support`;
  https://forums.opensuse.org/t/how-i-solved-the-opensuse-firefox-h-264-codec-headache/192767
- **Flathub:** https://flathub.org/setup/openSUSE. Check without root:
  `flatpak remotes`.
- **NVIDIA:** `SDB:NVIDIA_drivers`, snapshot 2026-03-11; G07 for Turing
  and newer, https://sndirsch.github.io/nvidia/2025/07/16/nvidia-drivers.html.
  Leap 16.0 installs the open driver on supported cards: release notes
  § 2.5, https://doc.opensuse.org/release-notes/x86_64/openSUSE/Leap/16.0/release-notes-leap-160.txt.
  Secure Boot needs a MOK enrolment at the next boot.
- **GPU detection without root:** `/sys/bus/pci/devices/*/class` and
  `vendor` are readable as a normal user (checked on this machine).
- **Microsoft fonts:** `fetchmsttfonts` is in Tumbleweed; not found in
  Leap 16.0's OSS repository.
- **Snapshots:** `snapper list-configs` works without root (checked on
  this machine).
- **Firewall and SSH:** `firewall-cmd --state` and `systemctl is-enabled`
  work without root (checked on this machine). Leap 16.0 release notes
  § 3.5: root password login over SSH is off on new installs.
- **YaST:** removed in Leap 16.0 (release notes § 3.9.1).
- **SELinux** is the default on Leap 16.0 and new Tumbleweed installs:
  https://news.opensuse.org/2025/02/13/tw-plans-to-adopt-selinux-as-default/
- **Broadcom Wi-Fi** needs `broadcom-wl` from Packman:
  https://forums.opensuse.org/t/after-tumbleweed-dup-broadcom-wi-fi-no-longer-works/173979
- **os-release `ID`:** `opensuse-tumbleweed`, `opensuse-slowroll`,
  `opensuse-leap` (15.x and 16.x share it; tell them apart by
  `VERSION_ID`), `opensuse-leap-micro`. From the release packages' spec
  files on build.opensuse.org.
